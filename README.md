# C-Level Hospital Executive & Financial Dashboard

[![Power BI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tool-PowerBI-orange?style=for-the-badge&logo=powerbi)](https://microsoft.com/power-bi)
[![DAX](https://img.shields.io/badge/Language-DAX-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://docs.microsoft.com/dax/)

Comprehensive, multi-page executive business intelligence dashboard designed for C-level management of Medical Park Hospitals. Built using Kaggle healthcare datasets to track financial performance, insurance management, clinical workflows, and operational efficiency.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Structure & Pages

1. **Ana Sayfa (Home):** Navigation hub and welcome interface.
2. **Yönetici Özeti (Executive Summary):** High-level overview of hospital KPIs, patient volume, and revenue streams.
3. **Hasta & Klinik Analizleri (Patient & Clinical Analytics):** Patient demographics, treatment frequencies, and clinical outcomes.
4. **Finans & Sigorta Yönetimi (Finance & Insurance Management):** 
   - Total Revenue, Average Billing, Denial Rates, Approval Rates, and Total Lost Revenue due to claims.
   - Provider cost distributions and denial reasons breakdown.
5. **Klinik & Personel Performansı (Clinical & Staff Performance):** Doctor workloads, patient-to-provider ratios, and departmental prescription density.
6. **Ameliyat & Operasyonel Verimlilik (Surgery & Operational Efficiency):** Procedure counts, surgical costs, lengths of stay, and procedural cost distributions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
- **Data Modeling:** Relationship mapping, handling one-to-many schemas, and star-schema optimization.
- **DAX Measures:** Complex calculated measures (e.g., Denial Rates, Custom Total Calculations, Dynamic Ratios).
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query for data cleansing, restructuring, and localization.
- **UI/UX Design:** Executive-ready card layouts, clean color palettes, and structured grid design.
